Brisbane

Learning to live in a different country combined with beautiful and traumatic memories caused this time to really shape how I see daily life. These photos will never explain how I see or experienced this period of time but do try to capture exploration and longing. Different emotions fueled these months and using my camera did feel like an escape when I needed it.
